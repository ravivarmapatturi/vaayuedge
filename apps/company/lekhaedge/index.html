LekhaEdge, document AI

Your paperwork, read without being uploaded.

Lekha means writing. LekhaEdge is being built to read documents and pull out the fields that matter on your own machine, so sensitive pages stay with you.

BankingInsuranceHealthcareLegalGovernment

Why on the device

These industries run on paper full of identity, health and financial details. Processing locally keeps those pages inside your own environment, which simplifies the question of who else has seen them.

How we are building it

No benchmark numbers yet. When we have measured results they will be published on the leaderboard, including where we lose.

Step 1

Benchmark small open OCR and layout models on printed English forms, then Indian-language documents.

Step 2

Ship a public demo: drop in a document and see extracted fields, with nothing uploaded.

Step 3

Choose one first document type and tune extraction for it.
