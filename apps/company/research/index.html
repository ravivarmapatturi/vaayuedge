2 Nov 2026

Should the live product change its translation model?

Not yet.

VaayuEdge currently uses Opus-MT because it runs in the browser. It scores well below IndicTrans2 on our benchmark, but we have not shown that a fine-tuned IndicTrans2 checkpoint can be exported and run in a browser with matching output.

The live product does not use the fine-tuned checkpoints below. Our direction is a much smaller model of our own, and we will report it here only once measured.
