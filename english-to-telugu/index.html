English-to-Telugu conversation with VaayuEdge

VaayuEdge (Vaayu Edge) combines English speech recognition, Telugu translation and speech output.

What VaayuEdge does

VaayuEdge is the first product from InferEdge AI. The conversation pipeline takes spoken English, recognizes the words, translates the resulting text into Telugu and produces Telugu speech. These are three distinct model stages, and an error in recognition can affect the translation that follows.

Try the current product

Open VaayuEdge using the product link below. Model downloads and startup time depend on your connection and device. Browser microphone permission is needed for speech input. Check the application's current mode and available controls rather than assuming every browser supports the same local execution path.

Privacy and offline goals

The project prioritizes inference on the user's own device where feasible. Local processing can reduce the need to transmit speech to an inference server. Offline availability depends on the chosen mode and whether required assets are already present; an initial model download can require internet access.

What makes Telugu translation difficult

Translation must preserve meaning, names, numbers, negation and the level of formality. Telugu fluency alone does not prove that a translation is faithful. Conversational phrasing can also differ from formal written Telugu, so evaluation should include native-speaker review alongside automatic metrics.

How we assess model changes

We compare upstream baselines and adapted models using complete evaluation references and recorded settings. A low training loss or a successful fine-tuning run does not establish better translations. Checkpoints must be benchmarked and reviewed before replacing the live product. Our current experiments are preliminary and do not establish state-of-the-art performance.

Device requirements and feedback

Available memory, CPU speed, acceleration support and browser compatibility influence usability. If recognition or translation fails, note the device, browser and example input when contacting us. Avoid sharing private recordings or personal information in public feedback.
