About InferEdge AI

InferEdge AI develops intelligence for devices with limited memory, compute and connectivity.

What we are building

InferEdge AI is an edge intelligence project exploring speech, language and vision models for laptops, phones, browsers and other constrained devices. Our focus is practical inference: the memory needed to load a model, the time required to produce useful output, and whether the experience works when connectivity is limited.

Our first product: VaayuEdge

VaayuEdge, also written Vaayu Edge, is our first product: an English-to-Telugu conversation experience combining speech recognition, translation and Telugu speech output. It is the starting point for a broader multimodal mission. Try the product to see its current functionality and device requirements.

How we evaluate progress

We separate a working demo from a measured model improvement. Translation experiments compare complete test sets with documented model versions, input data and decoding settings. Deployment evaluation must also consider process memory, latency, model download size and the complete speech pipeline. Current research does not establish universal superiority over other systems.

Model and data attribution

Our prototypes build on open models and datasets. An adapted checkpoint remains a derivative of its upstream model and must preserve relevant attribution and license notices. We distinguish our engineering and adaptation work from the architecture and data supplied by others.
