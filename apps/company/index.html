02 / Why the edge

Follow where your data goes.

The difference is the route. In one, your data makes a round trip. In the other, it never starts the journey.

Cloud AI

Needs a connection, adds a network trip to every request, and places your data on infrastructure you do not control.

On-device AI

The model downloads once. After that, inference happens on your hardware and your input does not need to go anywhere.

Private by design

Data that is never sent cannot be intercepted in transit or kept on someone else's server.

Fast without a network

No round trip means no waiting on signal, a real advantage in the places connectivity is weakest.

Cost that does not scale per call

The user's device does the work, so usage does not grow a GPU bill.
