VaayuEdge — English to Telugu speech translation on your device

Speak English and hear natural Telugu. VaayuEdge turns your speech into Telugu text and Telugu voice entirely on your device. Your voice is never uploaded, there is nothing to install, and it keeps working offline. VaayuEdge is a product of InferEdge AI, an edge AI company.

How it works

  1. Speak or type in English. On-device speech recognition (Whisper) converts your voice to text.
  2. Translate to Telugu. A compact neural translation model (Opus-MT) produces natural Telugu.
  3. Hear it in Telugu. A neural Telugu voice (Meta MMS-TTS) speaks the translation aloud.

Why on-device

  • Private: audio and text stay in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
  • Offline: after a one-time model download, translation works without internet.
  • Free: runs on the phone or laptop you already own, with no installation.

Frequently asked questions

Does VaayuEdge upload my voice?

No. Speech recognition, translation and Telugu speech synthesis all run locally in your browser.

Does it work on mobile phones?

VaayuEdge runs in modern browsers on laptops, desktops and recent phones. A one-time model download (about 500 MB) is needed on first use.

Is VaayuEdge free?

Yes, VaayuEdge is free to use.
