Model Scope, Accuracy & Operational Limits

Not for Unattended Legal or Financial Decisions: LekhaEdge is an on-device document intelligence beta. It is not certified for automated tax filing, unattended banking settlements, healthcare compliance, or legally binding contract verification. All extracted fields must be reviewed by a human operator.

Measured Benchmarks: Evaluated on the ICDAR 2019 SROIE receipt benchmark (50 test pages). Full page latency: 617 ms. Character Error Rate (CER): 0.3112. Word Error Rate (WER): 0.5819.

Zero Data Retention: Neither uploaded images, rendered PDF page rasters, nor extracted structured strings are ever sent across a network or written to local browser storage. Refreshing or closing the tab immediately purges all transient memory.
