About InferEdge AI

InferEdge AI develops intelligence for devices with limited memory, compute and connectivity.

Our mission

We work across speech and language for laptops, phones, browsers and other constrained devices. Useful results require measuring quality, runtime memory, response time and reliability in the intended environment.

Our first product: VaayuEdge

VaayuEdge is an English-to-Telugu conversation experience combining speech recognition, editable transcripts, translation and Telugu playback. It is the starting point for a broader multimodal mission.

How we report progress

We distinguish measured results, published third-party claims, targets and estimates. Current research does not establish universal superiority over other systems.
