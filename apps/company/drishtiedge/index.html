DrishtiEdge, vision AI

Vision that decides at the camera.

Drishti means sight. DrishtiEdge detects objects on the device that holds the camera, so video does not have to be streamed anywhere to be understood.

Why on the device

Video is heavy and often sensitive. Sending every frame to a server costs bandwidth, adds delay on a moving production line, and moves footage of people and premises off site.

How we are building it

The beta runs YOLOX-Nano (3.49 MB, Apache-2.0). Measured on 300 COCO val2017 images: mAP@0.5 of 0.42, about 76 ms per image in a desktop browser. It recognises 80 everyday object types and is not yet an inspection or surveillance product.

Step 1

Benchmark small open detection models in the browser and on a phone, on size, speed and accuracy.

Step 2

Ship a public demo that detects objects from your camera with nothing uploaded.

Step 3

Choose one first use case and train for it on licence-clean data.
