About

AI for devices with real limits.

InferEdge AI develops intelligence across modalities for phones, laptops, browsers and other constrained devices.

Our mission

Useful on-device AI has to fit the environment it serves.

We work across voice, vision and documents for devices with limited memory, compute, energy and connectivity. Quality matters alongside runtime memory, response time and reliability.

Our first product

VaayuEdge combines speech recognition, editable transcripts, translation and speech output. Its first focus is English and Telugu. English to Telugu is live today; Telugu to English is in development.

What comes next

Two more products are in development: DrishtiEdge for vision and LekhaEdge for documents.

How we report progress

We distinguish measured results, reproduced third-party results, published claims, targets and estimates. Current research does not establish universal superiority over other systems.
